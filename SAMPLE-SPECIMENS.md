## 3. Obsidian

- **Category:** Raw & Rough
- **Short summary:** Glossy black volcanic glass with a smooth natural fracture
- **Description:** Obsidian forms when silica-rich lava cools so quickly that crystals do not have time to grow. Its glassy surface and curved fracture lines record the rapid cooling of the volcanic melt. Handle sharp edges carefully if the specimen is freshly broken.
- **Formula:** Natural volcanic glass (composition varies; rich in SiO₂)
- **Hardness:** 5–5.5 (Mohs scale)
- **Locality:** Lipari, Aeolian Islands, Italy
- **Price:** 390
- **Image:** Choose a photo of an obsidian specimen from your computer.
- **Unsplash photo:** [Free obsidian photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-piece-of-shiny-green-obsidian-stone-LlWBe7i-FzU) — this is green obsidian. For a black specimen, browse [obsidian photos](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/obsidian) and choose a matching result.

## Add one to the site

1. Open the site's **Add a specimen** page.
2. Copy the fields for one specimen into the matching form fields.
3. Select a matching image on your computer.
4. Submit the form. The specimen should be saved to the database and appear in the collection.

Before choosing a photo on Unsplash, use its **Free** license filter because some results are Unsplash+ paid images. Open the photo page, download the image to your computer, then select that downloaded file in the specimen form. Unsplash's [license](https://unsplash.com/license) allows free use of its free images; giving the photographer credit is appreciated.

Mineral facts for the added examples: [Celestine](https://www.mindat.org/min-927.html), [Azurite](https://www.mindat.org/min-447.html), and [Rhodochrosite](https://www.mindat.org/min-3406.html).

## New category descriptions

- **Mineral Crystals & Clusters:** Natural mineral crystals, crystal clusters, and geodes displayed for their rock matrix, crystal shape, color, and growth patterns.
- **Rocks, Fossils & Geological:** Rocks, fossils, and geological keepsakes such as petrified wood, with their natural formation and origin recorded when known.
- **Carved Stones & Jewelry:** Mineral and rock pieces shaped into wearable jewelry or decorative carvings. Describe the stone and the craftsmanship separately.

### 10. Polished Petrified Wood Slice

- **Category:** Rocks, Fossils & Geological
- **Short summary:** Polished fossil wood showing preserved grain and mineral colors
- **Description:** This polished slice began as wood that was buried and gradually preserved as minerals filled pores and replaced parts of the original material. - **Formula:** Primarily silica (SiO₂) in many silicified examples; composition varies
